## Debian License

Debian is a free operating system whose components are all free software. The core licensing of Debian follows the [Debian Free Software Guidelines (DFSG)](https://www.debian.org/social_contract#guidelines).

### Main Licenses

- **Debian Core System**: Primarily under the GNU General Public License (GPL)
- **Debian Documentation**: Licensed under the [GNU Free Documentation License (GFDL)](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/fdl.html)
- **Packages in Debian Repositories**: Each package follows its respective upstream license (GPL, MIT, BSD, Apache, etc.)

### Mirror Content

The Debian mirror files (ISO) and package repositories provided on this site are identical to the official mirrors and have not been modified. All content licensing is subject to the official Debian terms.
